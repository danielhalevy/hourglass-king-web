The Hourglass King — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers the Android game The Hourglass King (package com.hourglass.king). It explains what the game stores and what third parties may collect when you play.

Information stored on your device

Your save games and game settings are stored on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games and keep cloud save on, a copy of your game progress is also stored in your Google account (Google Play Games Saved Games). We cannot see it; it is kept by Google under its own terms.

Google Play Games sign-in (optional)

The game has no account of its own. You may choose to sign in with Google Play Games. If you do, Google shares your Play Games player ID and player name with the game so it can save your progress to your Google account and restore your purchases. The Store in the game requires this sign-in; the story can always be played without it. We do not ask for your email address or any other personal details.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and use your advertising ID, device information (such as model, operating system and language), your IP address and approximate location, and how you interact with ads. Where required, the game shows Google's consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases and donations

The game offers optional in-app purchases (chests, coins, packs) and one-time supporter tips. Some chests give random items; the odds are shown in the game before you buy. All purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The game also may ask you, through Google Play's in-app review prompt, to rate it; that prompt is run by Google Play.

Selling data

We do not sell your personal data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Deleting your data

  • Cloud save: in the game open Settings → Cloud Save → Delete cloud save. This deletes your game progress from your Google account (you can also choose to delete this phone's saves).
  • Or email hourglasskingsupport@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data"; we will help you delete everything linked to the game within 30 days. Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required by law.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all local saves and settings from your device.
  • To stop ad personalization or reset your advertising ID, use the Ads settings in your Android device settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and the latest version will always be on this page.
